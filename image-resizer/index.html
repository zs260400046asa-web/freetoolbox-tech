Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about resizing images with this tool.

Is this image resizer really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no watermark, no daily limit and nothing to install. It is one of the free tools on FreeToolBox, supported by the site itself rather than by charging you per image.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The resizing is done by your own browser using the HTML canvas, on your device. Your image is never sent anywhere, which also means it works on private or personal photos safely.

Will resizing reduce the quality of my image?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp — this tool scales in steps for smoother results. Making an image much larger than the original can look soft, because no resizer can invent detail that was never there. For JPG and WebP you control the quality slider yourself.

What is the difference between resizing by pixels and by percentage?

Pixels give you an exact final size, which is what social platforms and websites ask for. Percentage is quicker when you just want the image half as big or twice as big without caring about the exact numbers. Both do the same job — the percentage slider simply works out the pixels for you.

Why does my PNG become a white background when I choose JPG?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas have to be filled with a solid color. The default is white, and you can pick any other background color in the format section before downloading.

What is the best size for an Instagram post?

1080 × 1080 pixels for a square post, 1080 × 1350 for a portrait post, and 1080 × 1920 for Stories and Reels covers. All three are one tap away in the presets and the size table above.
