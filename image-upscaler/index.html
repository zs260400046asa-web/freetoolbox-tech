Frequently asked questions

Is this image upscaler really free?

Yes. Upscaling is free with no signup, no watermark and no daily limit on this page, because the work happens in your own browser instead of on an expensive server.

What is the difference between 2× and 4×?

2× doubles both dimensions — a 800 × 600 image becomes 1600 × 1200. 4× makes it 3200 × 2400. Bigger is not always better: if the original is very blurry, 2× usually looks cleaner than 4×.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is processed locally in your browser tab and is never sent to a server. Closing the tab removes everything.

Why does my upscaled image still look soft?

Upscaling can sharpen edges and reduce pixelation, but it cannot recreate detail that the original never captured. Turn on Sharpen details and Smooth grain, and try 2× instead of 4× for the cleanest result.

Which format should I download?

PNG for maximum quality and transparency, JPG for the smallest everyday file, and WebP if the image is going on a website and you want quality with a small file size.
