Privacy Policy

FreeToolBox is a set of free image tools that run in your browser. This policy explains in plain words what we collect, and what we do not.

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Operated by: FreeToolBox Team

The short version

We do not collect your photos, your name, or your email. There is no account and no login. Your photos are processed on your own device and never leave it.

What this site does

FreeToolBox offers free image tools: a background remover, an image compressor, a resizer, an upscaler, and a photo editor. Every tool runs in your web browser. Nothing you open in a tool is sent to a server.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, no login — so no name, username, or password is ever asked for or stored.
  • No photos — images you open stay on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • No files on our servers — edited or downloaded images live only on your device.
  • No location data — we do not ask for or record where you are.
  • No payment details — every tool is free, so there is nothing to pay for.

Information we do collect

Almost nothing. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not share it with anyone.

Cookies

We do not use analytics cookies or advertising cookies, and we do not show ads. Our hosting setup may set one small security cookie that tells real visitors apart from automated abuse. That cookie does not track you and does not follow you to other sites.

Outside services

Two outside services are part of how the site works. Google Fonts supplies the text font for the pages. Hugging Face hosts the optional larger AI model for the background remover, which downloads in the background only if your connection allows it. Your photos are never sent to either service.

Children

The tools are free for everyone to use, and because there is no account, we do not collect age information. If you are a parent and you emailed us about your child, write to the address below and we will delete that email.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page and the effective date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email the FreeToolBox Team at soultech.aggency@gmail.com.
