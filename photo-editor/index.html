Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about editing photos with this tool.

Is this photo editor really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no watermark, no daily limit and nothing to install. It is one of the free tools on FreeToolBox, supported by the site itself rather than by charging you per photo.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The editing is done by your own browser using the HTML canvas, on your device. Your photo is never sent anywhere, which also means it works on private or personal photos safely.

What does Auto enhance do?

It applies a balanced lift: a little more brightness and contrast, a touch more colour, gentle warmth and light sharpening. It is a fixed recipe, not artificial intelligence, so the result is predictable. You can still move any slider after it to fine-tune the photo.

Will editing reduce the quality of my photo?

Edits are applied once, at download time, from your original photo — sliders do not stack damage on top of each other. PNG downloads are lossless. JPG uses the quality you pick, and WebP gives small files with very little visible loss.

Can I crop a photo to a square for a profile picture?

Yes. Pick Square in the crop section and the photo is cropped to the middle square. Then set the size to 1080 by 1080 pixels if you want a crisp profile photo, and download as PNG or JPG.

Why is my edited file bigger than the original?

Editing can add detail back into a heavily compressed photo, so the new file can grow. If that happens, this tool tells you and offers the original file for download instead, so you never get a bigger file by surprise.
